What this ranking is built from

Published for verification, not promotion. Every source below was checked against its own terms of use or robots.txt before being included or excluded — see the full trail in docs/source-policy/approved-sources.md. Last reviewed 2026-08-31.

Used in the score

  • Google Places — star rating and public review count.
  • County health department — public restaurant inspection records.
  • City open-data business license record — active status and tenure.
  • The restaurant's own website — used only to check that hours, menu, and contact info are current. Praise or testimonials a restaurant publishes about itself are not counted toward the score, since a business chooses what to display about itself and that isn't independent evidence.
    Not a sentiment input — see note below.
  • News and editorial coverage — found via a general search API, including local news station reporting, not limited to any one outlet.

Deliberately not used

  • Yelp and Tripadvisor — both restrict automated use of their data for a ranking product like this one in their own terms.
  • Better Business Bureau — its review and directory pages are closed to automated access.
  • Reservation-platform availability or "how many tables are open right now" — no platform offers this without a signed partnership agreement, and no compliant way to measure real-time demand independently exists yet. Checked, not guessed.
  • Any paid placement, subscription tier, or ad spend — there is no field for this in the underlying data model. A business cannot buy a higher score.

Why this page exists

Not every visitor needs to see this to use the ranking, which is why it isn't on the main pages — but nothing here is hidden. Anyone who wants to check that a source wasn't quietly excluded because it was inconvenient, or included because it was easy, can read exactly what was checked and why.
